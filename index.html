Project overview

A 200-room hotel on a 33,600 sq ft plot in G-11 Markaz, Islamabad. It has two basements, a ground-level restaurant and banquet complex, guest floors from the 3rd to the 8th, and a 9th-floor amenity level.

What the building contains

  • Guest accommodation: 200 rooms (King, Twin, Junior Suite, accessible), 26 with a courtyard view.
  • Restaurants and kitchens: all-day dining (220 seats), speciality restaurant, coffee shop, main and speciality kitchens, bakery.
  • Banqueting and meetings: 99 ft x 108 ft banquet hall with pre-function lobby, five meeting rooms and a board room.
  • Wellness: gym, spa (sauna, steam, treatment rooms), yoga room, swimming pool, kids pool and jacuzzi. Pool zone is on hold.
  • Back of house: laundry, stores, staff dining, offices, uniform room, prayer rooms.
  • Utilities: 155,000-gallon water tank, septic and diesel tanks, electrical substation, transformer room, pump room, AHU rooms.

Open points and things to check

These come from reading the tracker and the drawings against each other. Some may be recording gaps rather than real delays, and that is the first thing to confirm.

SequenceWaterproofing and caulking show 0% while block work is 100%. Washroom waterproofing normally follows block work and comes before tiling. Either it has not started, or it is not being recorded. Confirm which.
Sequence1st fix (conduits) shows 0% while plaster is 98%. Conduits are usually run before plaster closes the walls. Either the first fix is recorded in another place, or it is behind. Check with the MEP team.
GapFinishing and woodworks have no progress. Windows, marble sills, gypsum ceilings, washroom tiling, paint, doors, wardrobes and hardware all read 0% (or blank). Confirm whether this is real or not yet logged.

IMARAT Group

IMARAT is a Pakistani group active in real estate development, construction and building materials, hospitality, and property technology. Its main business in Islamabad is real estate, and it has built hotels with international chains.

  • Real estate development in the Federal Capital, including the Golf/Hills View luxury apartments with a country club.
  • Hospitality: hotels operated with Marriott International (Courtyard and Residence Inn) and Sheraton.
  • Construction and materials: a planned building-materials zone and the IMARAT Builders Mall in DHA Multan.
  • Property technology: Graana.com (online property marketplace), Agency21 (agency network) and PropSure Digital (verification and feasibility).
  • Education and philanthropy: a planned architecture and building-materials institute, and the Ali Akbar Foundation.

What to take with a pinch of salt

Much of the public information about IMARAT comes from IMARAT or its own companies. Read these points before quoting the group.

  • Graana.com, the source of much of the group's profile, is one of IMARAT's own companies, so its articles are self-published.
  • The group says it has over 2,000 staff and more than 50 offices. Neither figure was checked against an independent source.
  • The group's own pages describe its industry as "the only sector destined to change the destiny of our country". That is marketing language, not a finding.
  • The number of hotels is not consistent between sources. One press release lists Courtyard and Residence Inn properties in Islamabad and Multan. A company profile says two hotels were launched in Islamabad and Rawalpindi. The two lists do not match.
  • No public source found gives the G-11 hotel's room count, owner, or opening date. This site relies on the drawings and the tracker for those.

The Marriott partnership

IMARAT has signed franchise and development agreements with Marriott International for several hotels. In June 2021 IMARAT signed an MoU with principal builders to construct the Courtyard by Marriott in Islamabad. A 2023 press release describes IMARAT Hospitality as Marriott's partner for its hotels, which means Marriott's brand standards apply. The agreement terms are not public.
