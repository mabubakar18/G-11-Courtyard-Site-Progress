Project overview

A 200-room hotel on a 33,600 sq ft plot in G-11 Markaz, Islamabad. It has two basements, a ground-level restaurant and banquet complex, guest floors from the 3rd to the 8th, and a 9th-floor amenity level.

What the building contains

  • Guest accommodation: 200 rooms (King, Twin, Junior Suite, accessible), 26 with a courtyard view.
  • Restaurants and kitchens: all-day dining (220 seats), speciality restaurant, coffee shop, main and speciality kitchens, bakery.
  • Banqueting and meetings: 99 ft x 108 ft banquet hall with pre-function lobby, five meeting rooms and a board room.
  • Wellness: gym, spa (sauna, steam, treatment rooms), yoga room, swimming pool, kids pool and jacuzzi. Pool zone is on hold.
  • Back of house: laundry, stores, staff dining, offices, uniform room, prayer rooms.
  • Utilities: 155,000-gallon water tank, septic and diesel tanks, electrical substation, transformer room, pump room, AHU rooms.

IMARAT Group

IMARAT is a Pakistani group active in real estate development, construction and building materials, hospitality, and property technology.

  • Real estate development in the Federal Capital, including the Golf/Hills View luxury apartments with a country club.
  • Hospitality: hotels operated with Marriott International (Courtyard and Residence Inn) and Sheraton.
  • Construction and materials: a planned building-materials zone and the IMARAT Builders Mall in DHA Multan.
  • Property technology: Graana.com (online property marketplace), Agency21 (agency network) and PropSure Digital (verification and feasibility).

Notes on sources

  • Graana.com, a major source of the group's profile, is one of IMARAT's own companies, so its articles are self-published.
  • The group says it has over 2,000 staff and more than 50 offices. Neither figure has been checked against an independent source.
  • The hotel count differs between sources. Press releases list Courtyard and Residence Inn properties in Islamabad and Multan, and a company profile lists two hotels in Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
  • No public source found gives this hotel's room count, owner, or opening date. This site uses the drawings and tracker for those.

The Marriott partnership

In June 2021 IMARAT signed an MoU with principal builders to construct the Courtyard by Marriott in Islamabad. A 2023 press release describes IMARAT Hospitality as Marriott's partner for its hotels, which means Marriott's brand standards apply. The agreement terms are not public.
